Digital Personal Data Protection - Demo Excerpt
SYNTHETIC DEMO EXCERPT: paraphrased for a hackathon demonstration. Not the official text; clause numbering is illustrative. Verify against the official publication.
CHAPTER I Preliminary
1. This excerpt applies to the processing of digital personal data within India.
CHAPTER II Obligations of Data Fiduciary
2. A Data Fiduciary shall process personal data only for a lawful purpose for which the Data Principal has given consent or for certain legitimate uses.
3. Every request for consent shall be accompanied or preceded by a notice describing the personal data and the purpose of processing.
4. The Data Fiduciary shall give the Data Principal the option to access the notice in English or any language specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.
5. Consent shall be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with a clear affirmative action.
6. A Data Fiduciary shall enable the Data Principal to withdraw consent at any time, with the ease of doing so comparable to the ease with which consent was given.
7. A Data Fiduciary shall protect personal data by taking reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach.
8. In the event of a personal data breach, the Data Fiduciary shall intimate the Data Protection Board and each affected Data Principal.
9. A Data Fiduciary shall erase personal data when the purpose is no longer being served or consent is withdrawn, unless retention is necessary for compliance with any law.
10. A Data Fiduciary shall publish the business contact information of a person who is able to answer questions of the Data Principal about the processing of personal data.
11. Before processing personal data of a child, a Data Fiduciary shall obtain verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian.
12. A Data Fiduciary shall not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.
CHAPTER III Significant Data Fiduciary
13. A Significant Data Fiduciary shall appoint a Data Protection Officer based in India.
14. A Significant Data Fiduciary shall undertake a periodic Data Protection Impact Assessment and an independent data audit.
CHAPTER IV Rights of Data Principal
15. A Data Fiduciary shall, on request, provide the Data Principal with information about the personal data processed and the processing activities.
16. A Data Fiduciary shall give effect to a request of the Data Principal for correction, completion, updating and erasure of personal data.
CHAPTER V Data Protection Board
17. The Central Government shall establish the Data Protection Board of India.
