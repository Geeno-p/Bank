Information Security and Cyber Incident Management Policy
Bharat Unity Bank Ltd.
1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This Policy establishes the information security and cyber incident management framework of the Bank for all information assets, including those operated by service providers.
2. Governance
2.1 Board policy and CISO [ISB-C01]. The Board shall approve this Policy and the Bank shall appoint a Chief Information Security Officer reporting independently of the IT function. Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Annual.
3. Access Management
3.1 Role-based access and leaver revocation [ISB-C02]. Access to information systems shall be granted on a role basis, and access of leavers shall be revoked within one working day of exit. Control owner: Head of IT Operations. Frequency: Event Driven.
3.2 Quarterly review of internal privileged accounts [ISB-C03]. All internal privileged accounts shall be reviewed every quarter by the system owner. Control owner: System Owners. Frequency: Quarterly.
3.3 Multi-factor authentication [ISB-C04]. Multi-factor authentication shall be enforced for all privileged and remote access. Control owner: Head of IT Operations. Frequency: Continuous.
4. Technical Controls
4.1 Vulnerability management [ISB-C05]. Vulnerability assessments shall be conducted every quarter, and critical vulnerabilities shall be remediated within 15 days of identification. Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Quarterly.
4.2 Log retention [ISB-C06]. Logs of all ICT systems shall be maintained securely within Indian jurisdiction for a rolling period of 180 days. Control owner: Head of IT Operations. Frequency: Continuous.
4.3 Encryption standards [ISB-C07]. Data at rest shall be encrypted using AES-256 or stronger and data in transit shall be protected using TLS 1.2 or higher. Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Continuous.
5. Incident Management
5.1 Cyber incident reporting [ISB-C08]. Cyber security incidents shall be reported to CERT-In within six hours of being noticed and to the Reserve Bank of India within the timelines prescribed by it. Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Event Driven.
6. Awareness
6.1 Security awareness training [ISB-C09]. All employees shall complete information security awareness training annually. Control owner: Head of HR. Frequency: Annual.
