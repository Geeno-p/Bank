## 1. Purpose and Scope

**1.1** This Policy establishes the information security and cyber incident management framework of the Bank for all information assets, including those operated by service providers.


## 2. Governance

**2.1 Board policy and CISO [ISB-C01].** The Board shall approve this Policy and the Bank shall appoint a Chief Information Security Officer reporting independently of the IT function. _Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Annual._


## 3. Access Management

**3.1 Role-based access and leaver revocation [ISB-C02].** Access to information systems shall be granted on a role basis, and access of leavers shall be revoked within one working day of exit. _Control owner: Head of IT Operations. Frequency: Event Driven._

**3.2 Quarterly review of internal privileged accounts [ISB-C03].** All internal privileged accounts shall be reviewed every quarter by the system owner. _Control owner: System Owners. Frequency: Quarterly._

**3.3 Multi-factor authentication [ISB-C04].** Multi-factor authentication shall be enforced for all privileged and remote access. _Control owner: Head of IT Operations. Frequency: Continuous._


## 4. Technical Controls

**4.1 Vulnerability management [ISB-C05].** Vulnerability assessments shall be conducted every quarter, and critical vulnerabilities shall be remediated within 15 days of identification. _Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Quarterly._

**4.2 Log retention [ISB-C06].** Logs of all ICT systems shall be maintained securely within Indian jurisdiction for a rolling period of 180 days. _Control owner: Head of IT Operations. Frequency: Continuous._

**4.3 Encryption standards [ISB-C07].** Data at rest shall be encrypted using AES-256 or stronger and data in transit shall be protected using TLS 1.2 or higher. _Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Continuous._


## 5. Incident Management

**5.1 Cyber incident reporting [ISB-C08].** Cyber security incidents shall be reported to CERT-In within six hours of being noticed and to the Reserve Bank of India within the timelines prescribed by it. _Control owner: Chief Information Security Officer. Frequency: Event Driven._


## 6. Awareness

**6.1 Security awareness training [ISB-C09].** All employees shall complete information security awareness training annually. _Control owner: Head of HR. Frequency: Annual._
