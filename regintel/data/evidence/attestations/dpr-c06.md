# Control Attestation: DPR-C06 Children's personal data

- Policy: DPR-POL v2.0, clause 4.2
- Control owner: Head of Retail Banking
- Attestation period: 2024-04-01 to 2025-03-31
- Last design review of this control: 2024-09-30
- Attested on: 2025-03-31

The control owner confirms that the control below operated during the attestation period as documented, and that no exceptions were reported to the owner's knowledge.

> Before processing personal data of a child, the Bank shall obtain verifiable consent of the parent or lawful guardian and shall not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.

Signed: Head of Retail Banking (SYNTHETIC)
