# Control Attestation: ITO-C04 Audit and access rights in contracts

- Policy: ITO-POL v3.1, clause 4.1
- Control owner: Head of Legal
- Attestation period: 2024-04-01 to 2025-03-31
- Last design review of this control: 2024-03-28
- Attested on: 2025-03-31

The control owner confirms that the control below operated during the attestation period as documented, and that no exceptions were reported to the owner's knowledge.

> Every outsourcing agreement shall provide for the right of the Bank and the Reserve Bank of India, or persons authorised by them, to access and audit the service provider, together with clauses on confidentiality, sub-contracting and termination.

Signed: Head of Legal (SYNTHETIC)
